Every phone is a relay

A message to someone out of range is passed along by the phones in between. Each one keeps a copy for a while and hands it to every new neighbour, so it gets there even when people move.

  • Up to 7 hops across the mesh
  • Relays can’t read private messages — only pass them on
  • Signed receipts tell you when it’s delivered
  • Direct to a neighbour when they’re in range

Under the hood

How it works

01

Open the app

Your phone makes its own keys — no number, email or account. Pick a name and you’re on the mesh.

02

Phones find each other

Every phone is both a Bluetooth beacon and a listener, so anyone nearby running LibreChat joins in.

03

Messages hop

Out-of-range messages are flooded across the mesh, kept for up to a day, and acknowledged when they arrive.

Privacy

No servers. Nothing to hand over.

There is no LibreChat server and no account. Messages are encrypted on your phone, carried by other phones that can’t read them, and stored in an encrypted database.
